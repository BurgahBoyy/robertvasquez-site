Featured article

Robert Vasquez (born 2002) is an American entrepreneur based in Winter Garden, Florida. He graduated from Florida International University in 2023 in finance and information systems, entering with almost sixty credits and finishing two years early — while serving as president of his fraternity and taking a class of Mandarin. He has benched 300 pounds and is dying to go to a ballet. His musical range runs reggae to bossa nova to Frank Sinatra. He is not on social media and he despises it. His stated long-term project is the super network, which he describes as half real, half a bit, lowkey real. (Full article…)
