[edit]Is 300 lb verified?

The lead currently states a 300 lb bench press as fact. There is no video, no meet record, no spotter, and no witness on file. Under any reasonable sourcing standard this belongs in the body with attribution, not in the lead as a bare claim. — Bench300Truther (talk) 03:12, 4 August 2026 (UTC)

The subject is the source. The article says so. Attribution is already implicit. — WinterGardenEditor (talk) 08:40, 4 August 2026 (UTC)
