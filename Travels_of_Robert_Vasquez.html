Main article: Robert Vasquez

The travels of Robert Vasquez cover six countries outside the United States, concentrated in western Europe with one Caribbean entry. He has lived in Florida his entire life, which makes the travel record the only part of his biography that leaves the state.[1]

The record is also the strongest available support for his one claimed hidden talent, which is that he knows geography extremely well.[1]

[edit]Italy

Italy accounts for more of the record than any other country: Rome, Milan, Naples, the Amalfi Coast, Sardinia and the Genoa area.[1] The photograph used in the infobox of every article in this encyclopedia was taken on the Amalfi Coast, which makes Italy the visual default of RobPedia as well as its most-travelled entry.

[edit]London

London has been visited four times — twice as often as Paris and four times as often as anywhere else. No explanation for the frequency appears in the record.[1]

[edit]Switzerland

The Swiss entries are unusual in that Vasquez distinguishes between places he visited and places he passed through. Lucerne and Wengen count. Bern and Zurich, by his own account, he really just passed through.[1] The distinction is volunteered rather than requested, which is consistent with the geography claim.

[edit]Dominican Republic

The only entry outside Europe, and the only one with a documented group size: approximately thirty friends. Vasquez's assessment of the trip, in full, was “that was cool.”[1] It is the shortest review of anything in this encyclopedia.
