Main article: Robert Vasquez

The critical reception of Robert Vasquez is characterized by an unusually bimodal distribution. Across a sample of two reviews, ratings are split between the minimum and maximum available scores, producing an aggregate of 3.0 out of 5 that describes no actual reviewer's opinion.[1]

[edit]Analysis

The two reviews are not necessarily in conflict. Both describe an individual who produced a strong and durable impression; they differ on valence, not on magnitude.[2] No reviewer described him as forgettable, adequate, or fine. The corpus contains no three-star review and, per current editors, is unlikely to acquire one.

You'll never meet someone like him again.

His best friend, describing him as he puts him down on game, circa 2023[3]

Editors have noted that the above quotation is structurally identical to the five-star review and could, depending on delivery, also serve as the one-star review.[2]

[edit]Subject response

Vasquez has issued no response to either review. He has been observed reading both.[1] His only recorded comment on reception generally is the position that a strong negative reaction is preferable to no reaction, which editors have flagged as self-serving but not falsifiable.

[edit]Methodological limitations

  • Sample size of two.
  • Both reviewers anonymous.
  • Neither review dated.
  • Both reviews reproduced by the subject.
  • No control group.
