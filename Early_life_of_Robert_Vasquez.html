Early life of Robert Vasquez

From RobPedia, the free-ish encyclopedia

This article covers the early life of Robert Vasquez. For his adult career, see Robert Vasquez § Career.

The early life of Robert Vasquez spans his birth in 2002 through his departure from Miami, Florida. The period is generally regarded as formative, primarily by him.[1]

[edit]Family

Vasquez was born and raised in Miami. His grandparents are Cuban and Puerto Rican. He has described the resulting cultural position as “too white to be Hispanic, too Hispanic to be white,” a formulation he delivers without pausing for reaction.[1]

He has two younger brothers. The elder of the two, aged twelve, remains his primary Team Fortress 2 collaborator. His father, aged fifty-seven, plays Age of Empires II and, according to available accounts, is competitive at it.[1] The household also included a dog, who has no recorded position on any of this.

[edit]Miami

Growing up in Miami exposed Vasquez to the beach at an early age. He has since stated that he could be there all day, a claim that has not been falsified.[1] Sources indicate a persistent, low-intensity fantasy of tending bar at a beach club permanently — a plan he describes as low-key and has never once put a number to.

[edit]Reading and formation

Main article: The Giver

At twelve, Vasquez read The Giver. He credits the book with shaping his political views and his perspective on society.[2] Editors have noted that this is an unusually load-bearing role for one young-adult novel and have requested additional sourcing.

[edit]Education

Vasquez attended university, where he was elected president of his fraternity. During the same period he studied Mandarin. No source has explained how these two commitments coexisted; he maintains that they did.[1]
